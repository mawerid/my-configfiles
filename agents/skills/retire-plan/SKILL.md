---
name: retire-plan
description: Verify a finished plan against the delivered changes and retire its working document.
---

Locate the approved plan and its implementation range, including pending local changes. Verify every step and run final acceptance. Identify external checks awaiting evidence. Keep the plan open while acceptance is outstanding.
Have an independent reviewer compare requirements, commits, tests and docs when authorized. Resolve confirmed defects and rerun affected checks. Record judgment calls and remaining risks.
Work through the docs-to-update list. Reconcile architecture, decisions, glossary, roadmap and project instructions with actual behavior. Move durable decisions and deferred work to their respective homes. Archive or delete the plan according to project convention and deletion authorization. Report evidence, disposition and next roadmap item.
