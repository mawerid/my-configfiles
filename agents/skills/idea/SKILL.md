---
name: idea
description: Grill a proposal one decision at a time and turn the answers into a project idea.
---

Establish the problem, intended user, constraints and observable success. Read relevant code, existing decisions and backlog entries. Identify incompatible assumptions before recommending a design.
Compare viable options, including leaving things as they are. Explain costs, reversibility and what evidence would change the recommendation. Ask the highest-impact unanswered question first, give your recommendation and wait for the answer. Repeat until the decision is actionable.
Record the problem, options, tradeoffs, decision, open questions and owner in the project’s idea template or agreed notes. Link superseded backlog items. Hand an accepted idea to plan.

For an accepted idea, carry the decision and rationale into the plan or an ADR. Keep any unplanned milestones in the idea. Record a rejection with its reason in the backlog, or mark a parked idea for later consideration. Use the project's existing document locations and retention rules.
